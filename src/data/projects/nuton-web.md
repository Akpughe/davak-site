The customer-facing learning experience we are building at Nuton. Nuton Web is where learners bring in a YouTube video or document, turn it into an interactive course, and work through it with an AI tutor.

## The product

The app brings the whole learning loop into one place: course generation, explanations, quizzes, flashcards, and podcasts built around helping knowledge stick. Instead of treating a source file as a one-off chat prompt, Nuton Web turns it into a reusable learning environment.

## What we are building

We are focused on making the experience feel less like operating an AI tool and more like having a thoughtful study partner. The web app connects the learner-facing workflow to the AI systems behind Nuton, so generated material can be explored, practiced, and revisited over time.

This is the product surface where the work in Nuton RAG becomes useful: learners get a clear path from source material to understanding.
