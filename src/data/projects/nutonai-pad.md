Nuton’s internal product platform for turning AI-generated learning into usable school workflows. The codebase contains the teacher workspace, school portal, and admin application that sit around Nuton’s learning infrastructure.

## What it includes

The teacher workspace is designed around a simple flow: create, review, approve, and print or share lesson plans, lesson notes, assignments, classwork, and quizzes. The school portal gives schools a scoped way to onboard students and understand engagement, while the admin app handles the operational side of the platform.

The repository is a TypeScript monorepo with shared packages, a Supabase-backed data layer, server-side school boundaries, and the service contracts that connect the teacher experience to Nuton’s generation systems.
